### Scalability

- Scalability - Ability of a system to handle increasing workload efficiently.

---

### Horizontal Scaling

- Adds more servers or instances.
- Improves availability and fault tolerance.

---

### Vertical Scaling

- Increases CPU, RAM or storage of an existing server.
- Limited by hardware capacity.

---

### Stateless Application

- Does not store client session on the server.
- Easier to scale horizontally.

---

### Stateful Application

- Stores client session or state on the server.
- More difficult to scale.

---

### Auto Scaling

- Automatically adjusts resources based on workload.
- Optimizes cost and performance.

---

### Load Distribution

- Evenly distributes requests across multiple servers.
- Prevents server overload.

---

### Bottleneck

- Component that limits system performance.
- Should be identified and optimized.

---

### Elasticity

- Automatically increases or decreases resources based on demand.

---

### Capacity Planning

- Estimates future resource requirements.
- Prevents performance degradation.

---

### Benefits

- Better performance.
- Improved availability.
- Cost optimization.
- Supports business growth.

---

### Scale Up vs Scale Out

- **Scale Up** → Add more resources to one server.
- **Scale Out** → Add more servers.
