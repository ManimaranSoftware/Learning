### Requirement Gathering

- Understand functional and non-functional requirements before designing the system.

---

### Capacity Estimation

- Estimate users, requests, storage and bandwidth requirements.
- Helps choose the right architecture.

---

### High-Level Design (HLD)

- Defines major system components and their interactions.

---

### Low-Level Design (LLD)

- Defines classes, APIs, database schema and implementation details.

---

### Single Point of Failure (SPOF)

- Component whose failure can bring down the entire system.
- Should be eliminated using redundancy.

---

### Trade-Off

- Every design decision has advantages and disadvantages.
- Choose based on business requirements.

---

### Scalability

- Design the system to handle future growth.

---

### Availability

- Ensure the system remains operational even during failures.

---

### Consistency

- Ensure users receive accurate and valid data.

---

### Performance

- Optimize response time and resource utilization.

---

### Security

- Protect data and services using authentication, authorization and encryption.

---

### Monitoring

- Track application health, logs and performance metrics.

---

### Bottleneck Identification

- Find components limiting system performance.
- Optimize or scale them.

---

### Documentation

- Clearly document architecture, APIs and design decisions.

---

### Benefits

- Structured system design.
- Better scalability.
- Easier maintenance.
- Improved reliability.
- Better interview performance.

---
